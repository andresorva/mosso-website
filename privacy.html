Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026 · Effective: May 20, 2026

Mosso is built by an independent developer who respects your privacy. We collect the minimum data needed to keep the app reliable and improve gameplay. We do not sell your data. We do not show banner ads. We do not target children.

1. Who we are

Mosso is operated by DataNaat, based in Mexico City, Mexico. You can reach us at info@datanaat.com for any privacy-related question, request, or complaint.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

Mosso does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information to use the app. There are no name, email, phone, location, photo, or contact list inputs.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use Mosso, we collect limited technical and gameplay data through Google Firebase Analytics:

  • Device type (model, operating system, version)
  • App version and language
  • Approximate region (country level) inferred from your IP address — the IP itself is not stored
  • Session length and frequency
  • Anonymous events such as "game started", "level completed", "streak reached"
  • Crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics (technical diagnostics only)

This data is aggregated and used to understand which puzzles players enjoy, where the app crashes, and how to improve performance. It cannot be used to identify you personally.

2.3 Information stored on your device

Mosso stores your gameplay progress locally on your device using Hive, an encrypted local database. This includes your streak, completed levels, sanctuary collection, settings, and preferences. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up via a future cloud sync feature.

3. Third-party services

3.1 Google Firebase

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. Firebase is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can read more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3.2 Future advertising (planned for v1.1)

In a future update we plan to introduce optional rewarded ads: you may choose to watch a short video to recover a missed streak or get a hint. We will use Google AdMob with the strictest family-safe content filter ("G — General Audience"). Ads will never appear unless you actively choose to watch one. When ads are added, this policy will be updated and you will be notified inside the app.

3.3 Future in-app purchases (planned for v1.1)

Mosso will offer a one-time, optional purchase called "Mosso Forever" that disables rewarded ads. Payment is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your card information. Purchase records are managed through RevenueCat, which handles receipt validation only.

4. Children's privacy

Mosso is suitable for all audiences but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. We do not require sign-in, do not use behavioral advertising, and the future ads will use Google's child-safe filter.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at info@datanaat.com and we will respond promptly.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Union under GDPR or California under CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing
  • Withdraw consent for analytics (we are adding an in-app toggle in v1.1)

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@datanaat.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

You can also reset all locally-stored gameplay data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the "Reset progress" option in Settings.

6. Data retention

Aggregated Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months and then automatically deleted by Google. Crashlytics data is retained for 90 days. Gameplay data on your device is kept until you uninstall the app.

7. Data security

Local gameplay data is stored in an encrypted database on your device. Analytics data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. We follow industry-standard practices but no system is 100% secure; if you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

8. International data transfers

Mosso is operated from Mexico, and Firebase processes data on Google's servers, which may be located in the United States, the European Union, or other countries. By using Mosso you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes (such as adding rewarded ads in v1.1), we will notify you inside the app before the changes take effect.
